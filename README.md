# IT_125_Small_Business_Management_Project
IT 125 SQL Summer 2026

This database was designed to help a small business organize information
that is often scattered across spreadsheets, receipts, calendars, and
paper records. The design separates customers, vendors, products,
inventory, sales, expenses, and compliance records into related tables
to reduce duplicate data and improve consistency. Primary and foreign
keys were used to maintain relationships between tables and preserve
data integrity. An inventory table was included to better track stock
levels and reorder points independently of product information. Sample
data was added to demonstrate realistic business operations and support
testing. One challenge was balancing a simple design with enough detail
to support reporting, inventory management, and future expansion. The
final design emphasizes normalization, scalability, and ease of querying
while remaining appropriate for a startup business.
